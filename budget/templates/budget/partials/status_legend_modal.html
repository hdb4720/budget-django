Status Legend
Overdue
Budget was due in a past period and remains unpaid.
Due
Budget is due this period and not fully paid.
At Risk
Spending is approaching the budget limit.
Paid
Budget fully paid for this period.
Future
Budget is scheduled for a future period.
Partial
Budget partially paid but not complete.
No Activity
No budget and no transactions.
Actual Exists
Transactions exist but no budget was defined.
Transactional Only
Transient category has spending but no budget entry.
Diff ≠ 0
Paid-to-date does not match the budget amount.
Overspent
Actual spending exceeds the budget.
Above Historical
Spending exceeds historical maximum.
Below Historical
Spending is below historical minimum.
Missing Due Date
Budget entry exists but no due date was provided.
