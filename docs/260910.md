## Status rules
Planned status:
pending: due date set, in period, today or in the future; actual=0
partial: due date set, in period, today or in the future; actual<0
complete: due set, in period, due_date<=today
overpaid: due date set, in period; actual < amount
overdue: due date set, in period, in past; actual > amount
future: due date set, after period
 
Transient status:
under: actual > amount
met: actual = amount
over: actual < amount

incomplete: a budget with no due date, no budget with a due date
active: history with no budget
inactive: a budget with no history
idle: no budget, no due date

other: anything not covered above
